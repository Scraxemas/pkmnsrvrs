# v0.3.2 visual correction report

## Asset integration

- Audited the project, `D:\Projects`, and the local `pokemon-asset-master` registry for FRLG tiles, tilemaps, metatiles, and map atlases.
- No approved FireRed/LeafGreen environment artwork is locally available. The registry intentionally contains no Nintendo artwork and warns that decompilation source does not grant graphics redistribution rights.
- The currently loaded environment is the project-owned generated atlas at `public/assets/environment/viridian/forest-atlas-source.png`. It is explicitly not represented as FRLG artwork.
- `docs/visuals/current-environment-atlas-coordinates.png` records the current 8 × 8 atlas with `column,row` labels. `tools/audit_visual_assets.py` reproduces the sheet and sprite-padding audit.
- Remaining dependency: a user-supplied, documented, redistributable FRLG-compatible atlas. Until then, the authentic-art portion of the milestone is not complete.

## Terrain

- Root cause: rectangular path sprites and transparent edge cells were stamped without a semantic terrain layer or underlying grass, creating oversized crosses, checkerboard seams, and dark gaps.
- World generation now emits a deterministic 64-pixel grid with grass, path, and water cells. Main routes meander deterministically, spawn routes connect to them, and the center/flower areas form bounded clearings.
- Rendering computes an eight-neighbor mask per cell and selects straight edges, four outside corners, path interiors, shore edges, and water interiors. Every one of the 256 masks is regression-tested.
- Every transition is rendered above an opaque grass base, eliminating dark transparency seams.
- The current custom atlas does not contain verified art for every FRLG path ending, concave corner, T-junction, and intersection. The logical topology is ready, but authentic transition mapping remains part of the missing asset dependency.

## Pokémon grounding

- Root cause: the renderer used one global sprite origin and a hardcoded shadow offset, despite substantial species- and direction-specific transparent padding in PMD walk frames.
- `renderProfiles.ts` now defines scale, shadow size, optional elevation, and eight measured ground anchors per species. The actor's simulation coordinate is its ground-contact point.
- Shadows remain at the ground point. Butterfree and Beedrill use explicit visual elevation while their shadows stay grounded.
- Explicit profiles cover every loaded sheet, including the twelve required validation species.

## Environment collision and layering

- Tree visuals retain canopy size, while authoritative collision uses a smaller trunk radius.
- Y-depth remains based on each object's ground point, so Pokémon behind a tree are occluded by its canopy and Pokémon in front render above it.
- Water terrain and the ancient-tree trunk are impassable. Flowers and ground details remain non-blocking.
- The shared flow field, wall sliding, and crowd separation continue routing around the corrected collision geometry.

## HUD

- The player HP bar is fixed at the top with 3% side margins, 94% usable width, and a 32-pixel internal height.
- Healthy, warning, and critical states use green, amber, and red fills. HP is clamped visually and recalculates from authoritative current/max HP every render.
- Name, level, and numeric HP are centered in the bar. The information panel moved below it.
- Bosses receive a separate 70%-width bar beneath the player bar.
- Browser captures validate 1280 × 720 and 2560 × 1440 display sizes.

## Validation

- Vitest: 80 tests passed across nine files.
- Playwright Chromium: three scenarios passed, including grounding, terrain, pond, critical HP, and 2560 × 1440 HUD captures.
- World generation: forest, cave, and ship validate across 100 seeds each.
- Stationary-player navigation: 60 simulated seconds without hard lockup.
- Dense benchmark: 400 spawned enemies remain within the five-second budget.

## Known remaining visual defect

The running game still uses the project-owned custom forest atlas. It cannot honestly satisfy the requested FireRed/LeafGreen-art definition of done until an approved redistributable tileset is supplied. No unauthorized rip or decomp-derived graphic was downloaded or committed.

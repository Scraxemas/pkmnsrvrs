# v0.3.5 world-generation overhaul report

## Architecture

- Viridian Forest is now an 8192 × 8192 authoritative world divided into deterministic 512 px chunks.
- The renderer maintains a 5 × 5 active visual neighborhood and destroys distant chunk objects.
- Terrain and composition are reproducible from world seed and chunk coordinates.
- Large world bounds provide more than 4,000 px of travel in every direction from the starting point while streamed neighboring chunks hide finite boundaries during ordinary play.
- Decoration collision uses chunk buckets instead of scanning the entire world.

## Composition

Seven deterministic motifs replace uniform scatter:

- open clearings;
- forest walls;
- woodland corridors;
- clustered tall-grass fields;
- ponds;
- rocky patches;
- mixed landmark clusters.

Trees are placed as edge masses and groves. Tall grass uses elliptical multi-tile patches. Ponds use coherent water regions with FRLG shoreline transitions. Rocks, logs, stumps, flowers, and sparse ground details provide secondary structure.

Two continuous analytic routes cross chunk boundaries without requiring every chunk to contain a giant intersection. The central start area is protected from obstacle placement, while dynamic enemy spawning samples a deterministic ring around the player and rejects blocked points.

## Validation

- Chunk generation determinism.
- Path continuity across chunk seams.
- Tall-grass clustering.
- Per-chunk obstacle-density limits.
- Safe central spawn area.
- Stationary-player navigation and 100/250/400 enemy scenarios.
- Playwright screenshots for clearing, forest wall, tall grass, pond, rocks, corridor, and long-distance streamed travel.
